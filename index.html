Where these came from

The shelf started with 30 formats and grew from what corporate L&D teams, facilitators, tool makers, and AI adoption programs describe using. No list is ever complete: facilitation books like Gamestorming hold 80+ more workshop techniques, many of them meeting methods rather than games. Cards link to the examples behind them.

    "Used recently" marks are saved in this browser only. Group sizes and times are starting points, not limits.
